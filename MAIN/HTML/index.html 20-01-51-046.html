---Penne Pomodoro Recipe---

By Noah C Davison

Photo Of Penne Pomodoro For Reference

Thank you for checking out my recipe for Penne Pomodoro, a traditional italian dish!

Before you begin, you'll need the following ingredients/utensils:

  • [ ]Penne Pasta Noodles
  • [ ]Tomato Sauce or Marinara
  • [ ]One Roma or Beefsteak Tomato
  • [ ]Garlic Powder or Diced Garlic
  • [ ]One Large Pot for Noodles
  • [ ]One Small Saucepan for Sauce
  • [ ]Large Ladle/Spoon for Sauce
  • [ ]Tongs/Large Fork for Noodles
  • [ ]One Large Strainer

Once you've gathered the ingredients, and have prepared your pot/saucepan, You're ready to start cooking!

  1. Step One: Boil a pot of water on medium/high heat.
  2. Step Two: Dice your tomato, garlic, and mix into tomato sauce.
  3. Step Three: Add penne pasta noodles to pot of water, stirring frequently until noodles are almost soft.
  4. Step Four: Strain water from noodles, then rinse noodles with cold water.
  5. Step Five: Pour Tomato Sauce/Garlic mixture into small saucepan, and heat on medium/low heat until bubbling.
  6. Step Six: Add your washed penne noodles to bubbling saucepan, slowly stirring into mix.
  7. Step Seven: After several minutes, remove saucepan from heat, and pour it all into your bowl!

Lastly, Enjoy! ^_^
