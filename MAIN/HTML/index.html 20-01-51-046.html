Penne Pomodoro Recipe

Photo Of Penne Pomodoro For Reference

Hey there! This is my recipe for penne pomodoro, a traditional italian dish.

For this recipe you'll need the following items:

  • Penne Pasta Noodles
  • Tomato Sauce
  • One Roma or Beefsteak Tomato
  • Garlic Powder or 1 Fresh Garlic Clove
  • One Large Pot
  • One Small Saucepan
